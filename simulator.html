How the simulator works

  1. Geometry. GSD = h·p/f for each photo, using the altitude the drone actually held. Footprint = sensor pixels × GSD.
  2. Blur. Ground smear = speed × exposure. Resolution along the flight line is combined with GSD, which lowers pixels across the lesion at high speed.
  3. Workload. Tiles per frame follow eq 3 on the image fed to the model. Time per frame = overhead + tile work ÷ tiles/s, with lognormal jitter matched to your p95/p50.
  4. Flight. The drone accelerates and brakes at the set limit on each lane and lane change. Altitude wanders with the hold error you set.
  5. Compute. Photos enter a 3-frame buffer. A full buffer drops the photo, and lesions under it go unanalysed unless an overlapping photo is analysed.
  6. Detection. Your measured recall-vs-pixels curve if given, otherwise model recall × a logistic curve.
  7. Power. Rotor momentum theory with drag: induced power ÷ figure of merit, plus drag × airspeed, ÷ motor efficiency. Airspeed includes wind, so lanes into the wind cost more. One measured hover power rescales the whole curve.

Still simplified: flat terrain, nadir camera, uniform light, no gusts or occlusion. Processing is serial: overhead and inference do not overlap. Label outputs SIMULATION until the inputs above are measured.
